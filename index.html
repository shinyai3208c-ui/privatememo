Questions

How is this different from Notion or Obsidian?

They sync your notes to their servers (or make you run your own). We run entirely on your device. The AI that organizes your notes is installed on your phone and never sees the internet.

Does it need internet to work?

No. Capture, organize, and search all work offline. The app doesn't request network permission at all.

What if I lose my phone?

You can export your notes as plain files to your own storage anytime. No cloud required.

Which languages?

English at launch, more languages as we ship. The on-device model already handles multilingual notes.
